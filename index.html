How to Play

Tap one tile, then tap a neighboring tile to swap. Match 3 or more berries to clear them, earn XP, build streaks, and trigger stronger chain reactions.

Berry Booms clear every matching berry on the board. Mystery Berry Box and random drop rewards can give you extra Berry Booms to store for later.

Shake Up refreshes the board whenever you want a new layout.

Quick Answers

How do I refresh the board? Use Shake Up from the bottom menu or the Shake Up image button in the play scene.

What is a streak? Every successful match move increases your streak. A failed swap resets it.

How do Berry Booms work? Tap a stored Berry Boom, confirm it, and it will explode all of that berry type from the board.

Where is my progress saved? Your profile and progress are stored locally on this device.
